# Buna: Learn Languages

**Learn a language a few words at a time, without opening an app.**
**Aprende un idioma unas pocas palabras cada vez, sin tener que abrir la app.**

Buna is a language-learning app for Android built around one simple idea: an **alarm that teaches you**. At the times you choose, Buna rings with a new word and an example sentence, so you learn in small moments of your day. When you want to study more, you can practice at your own pace.

Buna es una app para aprender idiomas en Android basada en una idea sencilla: una **alarma que te enseña**. A las horas que elijas, Buna te muestra una palabra nueva con su frase de ejemplo, para que aprendas en pequeños momentos del día. Cuando quieras estudiar más, practicas a tu ritmo.

## Features / Funciones

- **Learning alarms / Alarmas que enseñan** – choose how often they ring; each one shows a word and a sentence.
- **Free practice / Práctica libre** – learn new words in short rounds: see the word, order the sentence, listen, and write it.
- **Practice by category / Práctica por categoría** – study vocabulary by topic: food, travel, work and more.
- **Verb conjugations / Conjugaciones** – practice verbs in the present, past and future, with a help table and a review of your mistakes.
- **Roadmap / Camino** – see your progress level by level (A1, A2, B1) in blocks of words.
- **Read aloud / Lectura en voz alta** – hear words and sentences with your phone's voice.
- **Streaks and achievements / Rachas y logros** – stay motivated every day.
- **Private / Privado** – no account, no ads and no data sent anywhere: your progress stays on your phone.

## Languages / Idiomas

- **App interface / Interfaz:** Español, English, Português, Français.
- **Languages you can learn / Idiomas que puedes aprender:** English, Español, Português, Français, Deutsch, Italiano — up to 1,400 words each, with example sentences.
